Last updated: 5 October 2026

This notice describes information handling on the erbhq.com informational website only. The website is operated by Dineshbabu Elumalai, an individual using the name “ERB.” This notice does not cover LaunchProof or other linked products, which may have their own policies.

1. Information you provide

When you submit the ERB contact form, we receive your name, work email, and enquiry. If you choose to provide them, we also receive your company, website or App Store URL, current iOS stack, and timeline. Your browser sends these details to a Cloudflare Pages Function, which uses Resend to send the enquiry to the operator’s email inbox. We do not require an account or use a database for form submissions. If you email hello@erbhq.com directly, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards that message to the operator’s inbox.

2. Please avoid confidential information

Do not send source code, credentials, passwords, payment details, or other confidential or sensitive information through the initial enquiry form. Repository access is not requested through this website.

3. How information is used

Information you choose to send may be used to read and reply to your enquiry and, if relevant, discuss a possible technical review or engineering engagement. An enquiry does not create a client relationship.

4. Hosting, technical data, and analytics

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses, request data, and security events to serve and protect the website, according to its own practices. The contact form uses a Cloudflare Pages Function. This site’s source code does not add advertising pixels or a separate analytics script. Cloudflare and Resend may process information needed to provide their services under their own policies.

5. Service providers and retention

Cloudflare processes web requests and incoming email forwarding; Resend processes contact-form email delivery; and the email provider holds received messages. ERB does not save form submissions to an ERB database. Messages can remain in email inboxes and provider logs under their respective retention practices. Enquiries are reviewed and deleted when no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations.

6. Your requests

To ask about information you have sent, including applicable access, correction, or deletion requests, use the address below. Some information may need to be retained where law requires it.

7. External links and other products

Links to the operator’s portfolio, LaunchProof, or other sites lead to separate services with their own practices and privacy notices. This notice applies only to erbhq.com.
