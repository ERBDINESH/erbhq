YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This notice describes information handling on the erbhq.com informational website only. The website is operated by Dineshbabu Elumalai, an individual using the name “ERB.” This notice does not cover LaunchProof or other linked products, which may have their own policies.

1. Information you provide

When you submit the ERB HQ contact form, we receive your name, email address and project message. Your browser sends these details to a Cloudflare Pages Function, which uses Resend to send the enquiry to our operator’s email inbox. We do not require an account or use a database for form submissions. If you email hello@erbhq.com directly, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards that message to our operator’s inbox. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry.

2. How information is used

Information you choose to send may be used to read and reply to your enquiry and, if relevant, discuss a possible collaboration. An enquiry does not create a client relationship.

3. Hosting, technical data and analytics

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses, request data and security events to serve and protect the website, according to its own practices. The contact form uses a Cloudflare Pages Function. This site’s source code does not add advertising pixels or a separately installed analytics script. Cloudflare and Resend may process information needed to provide their services under their own policies. If other integrations are enabled, this notice will be updated.

4. Service providers and retention

Cloudflare processes web requests and incoming email forwarding; Resend processes contact-form email delivery; and our email provider holds received messages. We do not save form submissions to an ERB HQ database. Messages can remain in email inboxes and provider logs under their respective retention practices. We review and delete enquiries when no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations. You can request information or deletion using the address below.

5. Your requests

To ask about information you have sent, including applicable access, correction or deletion requests, use the contact link below. Some information may need to be retained where law requires it.

6. External links and other products

Links to the operator’s portfolio, LaunchProof or other sites lead to separate services with their own practices and privacy notices. This notice applies only to erbhq.com.

7. Changes

This notice may be updated as the website changes. The update date will appear on this page.

8. Contact and operator

Operator: Dineshbabu Elumalai (individual operating under the ERB name). For website privacy enquiries, email hello@erbhq.com.
